# **# LUCID: Reality? — Project Vision**



### **## What is Artificial Reality?**



##### Artificial Reality is a new category of human experience 

##### that sits beyond Virtual Reality and Augmented Reality.

##### 

##### While VR delivers computer-generated images through a 

##### headset, and AR overlays digital content on the physical 

##### world, Artificial Reality generates experience directly 

##### within human consciousness through the dream interface.

##### 

##### It is not display technology. It is consciousness technology.



### **## The LUCID pipeline**



##### The dream state is the only natural state where the human 

##### brain generates a complete, immersive reality indistinguishable 

##### from waking experience — without any external display.



##### LUCID: Reality? aims to interface with this state:

##### 

##### 1\. Detect when a person enters REM sleep

##### 2\. Induce lucid awareness (consciousness within the dream)

##### 3\. Stream AI-generated world content into the dream experience

##### 4\. Enable multiple users to share the same dream world

##### 5\. Create a persistent Artificial Reality accessible through sleep

##### 

### **## Why now?**



##### Five converging technologies make this achievable 

##### in the next 10–15 years:

##### 

##### \- High-resolution consumer BCI (accelerating)

##### \- Real-time generative AI (largely solved)

##### \- Neural rendering for 3D worlds (near-term)

##### \- Validated lucid dream induction (this project)

##### \- Consumer compute for real-time inference (3–5 years)

### 

### **## Current status**



##### Project 07 is the first component: automated REM detection 

##### and closed-loop haptic stimulation for lucid dream induction.

##### 

##### Results to date:

##### \- CNN-LSTM classifier: 80.14% accuracy on Sleep-EDF

##### \- REM F1-score: 0.81

##### \- Closed-loop hardware trigger: confirmed working



### **## Timeline**



##### Phase 1 (2024–2026): BCI classifier + lucid induction

##### Phase 2 (2026–2028): Neural decoding + image generation  

##### Phase 3 (2028–2031): 3D world generation + navigation

##### Phase 4 (2031–2036): Multi-user shared Artificial Reality

##### Phase 5 (2036+):     Full Artificial Reality platform



### **## The name**



##### \*\*LUCID: Reality?\*\* — because the question mark is intentional.

##### When you are inside Artificial Reality, the question 

##### "is this real?" becomes genuinely unanswerable.



##### That is the goal.
